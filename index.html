About Me

A little introduction about my professional journey

Hello! I am Mahesh Arde, a Bachelor of Computer Applications graduate with a strong interest in Linux System Administration and IT infrastructure.

I have practical experience working with Linux environments including RHEL, CentOS and Ubuntu. My technical skills include user and permission management, LVM, SSH, networking, service management, system troubleshooting and server monitoring.

I also work on Bash scripting and Ansible automation to simplify routine administration tasks. I have completed RHCSA training and hold the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification.
